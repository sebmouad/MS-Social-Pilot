# Workspace data controls

The Data and privacy settings page exports a JSON snapshot of workspace details, memberships, posts and destination links, connection metadata, inbox history, and stored Page and ad metrics. It omits provider access and refresh tokens and the instance-wide `root_activities` audit history. The export is generated on request, sent with `no-store`, and is not persisted by the app.

Deleting workspace data requires an owner or admin and the exact confirmation `DELETE`. Before confirmation, the page requests a read-only count preview for posts, post links, comments, destinations, conversations and messages, social and advertising account records, and stored page and ad insights. The preview is an estimate from the time it was requested; the actual delete runs in one transaction and returns counts for rows removed. Workspace identity and memberships remain. The audit event with aggregate record counts is written in the transaction after removal. Download an export before deletion when a recovery copy is needed.

Coverage is limited by `app/rebuild/prisma/schema.prisma`, which is a partial compatibility view rather than a complete migration schema. Legacy workspace data outside that view is not included in the preview or export. The main legacy schema includes additional tables such as `page_reviews`, `external_posts`, `page_videos`, `instagram_accounts` and their media/insights, `inbox_notes`, `post_versions`, `post_notes`, `workspace_exports`, and other workspace settings and operational records. Some rows may be removed by database foreign-key cascades when mapped targets/posts are deleted; their counts are not included here. Storage files referenced by workspace exports are also outside this deletion path. A complete erasure requires a full relation inventory and explicit handling of those tables and files.

Disconnecting a social source offers two local choices. Retaining history disables its channels and ad accounts while keeping records. Delete mode first previews counts for mapped channels, ad accounts, insights, inbox messages and comments, destination links, and posts that will become orphaned. It removes those mapped records; a post with links to other channels remains. The preview is an estimate and does not count cascaded rows in unmapped legacy tables. Delete mode is blocked while posts remain scheduled at the provider. Neither choice removes remote posts or revokes authorization at the provider. The choice and actual affected counts are audited.
