# UI/UX audit — 2026-10-24

Status: **Batch 1 (quick fixes, items 1–6) and Batch 2 (consistency, items 7–12) implemented and unit/typecheck verified.** Browser re-check (`scripts/dev/ui-audit.mjs`) and release remain for the preview/deploy lane. Each item below was
confirmed on a screenshot. Items marked "source checked" also have a confirmed
root cause in the code.

## Evidence and method

- Local preview (synthetic seed data, dedicated database), dev server on
  `http://localhost:3300`. Database and seed setup are in [PREVIEW.md](PREVIEW.md).
  `scripts/dev/qa-db.sh` rebuilds the database.
- `scripts/dev/ui-audit.mjs` signs in as the preview QA user. For each route it
  takes a full-page screenshot and records DOM checks:
  - unnamed controls and unlabelled inputs;
  - horizontal overflow and elements past the right edge;
  - tap targets under 32px on phone;
  - raw `undefined`/`NaN` text and console errors.
- Coverage: 37 customer and Root routes at 1440px and 390px (75 captures).
- No page overflows horizontally at either width. The "unnamed switch" hits
  were false positives: `Switch` is labelled through `<label for>`.
- Preview-data artifact, not a bug: every composer channel shows "Reconnect",
  because the fake credential cannot be decrypted.

## Batch 1 — quick fixes (implemented)

1. **Sidebar channel names clip without an ellipsis**, and the "All channels"
   highlight overruns its column. *Implemented:* constrained `.shell-sidebar__scroll`,
   `.shell-hubs`, `.shell-channels`, `.ui-scope`, `.ui-scope__list`, `.ui-scope__row`,
   and `.ui-scope__item` with `grid-template-columns: minmax(0, 1fr)` / `min-width: 0` /
   `overflow: hidden` in `src/styles/shell.css`, and added `scopeActive` to
   `ChannelScopeList` so "All channels" is only highlighted on channel-scoped areas.
2. **Approvals board overflows at 1440px.** *Implemented:* changed `.board` in
   `publishing/ui/approvals/approvals.module.css` to
   `repeat(auto-fit, minmax(min(100%, 260px), 1fr))` with a 2-column wrap at `1400px`.
3. **Tasks and Agency show another area's tabs with nothing selected.**
   *Implemented:* `AppShell.tsx` computes `effectiveOptionalAreas` so the active
   optional area (`tasks` or `agency`) is always included in `hubPages`, keeping
   its tab visible and selected.
4. **AI studio shows tabs that lead to "This is not available for your
   workspace right now."** *Implemented:* `AiStudioScreen.tsx` filters out
   disabled tools from the tab strip, renders the unavailable `EmptyState` only
   when all four tools are disabled, and `AiStudioPage.tsx` redirects stale
   `?tab=` values to the first available tool.
5. **Plurals.** *Implemented:* added singular/plural EN and FR copy keys for
   Agency (`channel cannot publish`, `failed post`, `overdue task`), Publishing
   (`contentCountOne`, `calendarLanePostsOne`), and Ads Manage (`resultOne`,
   `results ·` / `résultats ·`).
6. **Raw formats.** *Implemented:* formatted Ads Manage and Compare currencies
   with `Intl.NumberFormat(locale, { style: 'currency', currency })`, formatted
   ISO `YYYY-MM-DD` dates with `Intl.DateTimeFormat(locale, { dateStyle: 'medium', timeZone: 'UTC' })`,
   and formatted single-day `TrendChart` bar categories with `dateFormatters(...).axis`.

- **Horizontal overflow:** 0 on all 37 routes.
