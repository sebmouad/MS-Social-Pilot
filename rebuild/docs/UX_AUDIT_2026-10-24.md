# UI/UX audit — 2026-10-24

Status: **Batch 1 (quick fixes, items 1–6) and Batch 2 (consistency, items 7–12) implemented and unit/typecheck verified.** Browser re-check (`scripts/dev/ui-audit.mjs`) and release remain for the preview/deploy lane. Each item below was
confirmed on a screenshot. Items marked "source checked" also have a confirmed
root cause in the code.

## Evidence and method

- Local preview (synthetic seed data, dedicated database), dev server on
  `http://localhost:3300`. Database and seed setup are in [PREVIEW.md](PREVIEW.md).
  `scripts/dev/qa-db.sh` rebuilds the database.
- `scripts/dev/ui-audit.mjs` signs in as the preview QA user. For each route it
  takes a full-page screenshot and records DOM checks:
  - unnamed controls and unlabelled inputs;
  - horizontal overflow and elements past the right edge;
  - tap targets under 32px on phone;
  - raw `undefined`/`NaN` text and console errors.
- Coverage: 37 customer and Root routes at 1440px and 390px (75 captures).
- No page overflows horizontally at either width. The "unnamed switch" hits
  were false positives: `Switch` is labelled through `<label for>`.
- Preview-data artifact, not a bug: every composer channel shows "Reconnect",
  because the fake credential cannot be decrypted.

## Batch 1 — quick fixes (implemented)

1. **Sidebar channel names clip without an ellipsis**, and the "All channels"
   highlight overruns its column. *Implemented:* constrained `.shell-sidebar__scroll`,
   `.shell-hubs`, `.shell-channels`, `.ui-scope`, `.ui-scope__list`, `.ui-scope__row`,
   and `.ui-scope__item` with `grid-template-columns: minmax(0, 1fr)` / `min-width: 0` /
   `overflow: hidden` in `src/styles/shell.css`, and added `scopeActive` to
   `ChannelScopeList` so "All channels" is only highlighted on channel-scoped areas.
2. **Approvals board overflows at 1440px.** *Implemented:* changed `.board` in
   `publishing/ui/approvals/approvals.module.css` to
   `repeat(auto-fit, minmax(min(100%, 260px), 1fr))` with a 2-column wrap at `1400px`.
3. **Tasks and Agency show another area's tabs with nothing selected.**
   *Implemented:* `AppShell.tsx` computes `effectiveOptionalAreas` so the active
   optional area (`tasks` or `agency`) is always included in `hubPages`, keeping
   its tab visible and selected.
4. **AI studio shows tabs that lead to "This is not available for your
   workspace right now."** *Implemented:* `AiStudioScreen.tsx` filters out
   disabled tools from the tab strip, renders the unavailable `EmptyState` only
   when all four tools are disabled, and `AiStudioPage.tsx` redirects stale
   `?tab=` values to the first available tool.
5. **Plurals.** *Implemented:* added singular/plural EN and FR copy keys for
   Agency (`channel cannot publish`, `failed post`, `overdue task`), Publishing
   (`contentCountOne`, `calendarLanePostsOne`), and Ads Manage (`resultOne`,
   `results ·` / `résultats ·`).
6. **Raw formats.** *Implemented:* formatted Ads Manage and Compare currencies
   with `Intl.NumberFormat(locale, { style: 'currency', currency })`, formatted
   ISO `YYYY-MM-DD` dates with `Intl.DateTimeFormat(locale, { dateStyle: 'medium', timeZone: 'UTC' })`,
   and formatted single-day `TrendChart` bar categories with `dateFormatters(...).axis`.

## Batch 2 — consistency (implemented)

7. **One period control.** *Implemented:* added `365` to
   `analytics.insightsPolicy.rangePresets`, standardized `/insights/audience`
   and `/insights/posts` on `InsightsFilterRow` (`DateRangePicker`), and updated
   `/insights/compare` to default to the 28-day policy window vs the preceding
   28-day window while using `DateRangePicker` for both periods.
8. **Filters.** *Implemented:*
   - Replaced Search + Apply with live search in Library (`MediaLibrary.tsx`),
     Ads Manage (`ManageSearchFilter.tsx`), and Inbox (`InboxFilterRow.tsx`).
   - Converted `InboxSavedViews.tsx` into a compact `Views ▾` popover menu on
     the filter toolbar.
   - Excluded the default `kind === 'message'` from active filter chips on `/inbox`.
9. **One primary button per view.** *Implemented:* changed the account-card
   `Reconnect` button in Settings › Channels (`row-menu.tsx`) to
   `variant="secondary"` so `Manage selections` is the sole primary CTA, and hid
   the duplicate toolbar `New task` button on `/tasks` when the unfiltered empty
   state is shown (`TaskBoard.tsx`).
10. **Shared components.** *Implemented:* styled the Workspace "Link tagging (UTM)"
    `<details>` disclosure summary in `settings/ui/workspace/cards.module.css`
    and replaced raw native sort/direction `<select>` controls on `/insights/posts`
    with `.ui-input`-styled `AddressSelect` controls.
11. **Disclaimers behind ⓘ.** *Implemented:* moved the `CsvExportButton`
    export note, Campaigns per-account hierarchy/objective/funnel notes
    (`CampaignsScreen.tsx`, `CampaignCharts.tsx`), and Settings › Channels
    footer note (`ConnectionsScreen.tsx`, `_components.tsx`) into `InfoTip` (`ⓘ`)
    triggers beside their headings/actions.
12. **Agency KPI tiles.** *Implemented:* widened `.metrics` grid min column width
    to `240px`, prevented desktop wrapping in `.actions` (`agency.module.css`),
    and updated `--mod-agency` across all theme palettes in `src/styles/tokens.css`
    from pink to pairwise-distinct brand-blue/indigo shades that pass contrast checks.

## Phone (390px) findings, not yet assigned to a batch

- **Tap targets under the 40px coarse-pointer goal:**
  - native checkboxes and radios at 13–16px (Queue, Team, Integrations scope
    matrix, Content feeds, Insights Compare);
  - inline text links at 16–20px ("Recipe planner", "Queue automation",
    "Choose ad accounts", "Open groups", "Review sent/failed posts",
    "Reset filters", Root sidebar links);
  - ⓘ buttons at 24px;
  - calendar Month/Week/List at 30px and event links at 28px.

  A shared coarse-pointer rule is the likely fix.
- **Ads › Analysis shows raw JSON.** The "Stored ratios, actions and
  additional fields" column renders a JSON `<pre>` to customers
  (`analytics/ad-objects/Screen.tsx` ~75). Use a labelled list instead.

  - one brand "today" marker;

  - a realistic neutral post preview built from module preview metadata;
  - visible reasons for blocked channels.

- **Insights:**
  - empty and partial states on Overview;
  - Top posts duplicates;
  - a single day drawn as a bar;
  - human audience labels;
  - Posts metric columns with sortable headers.

- **Library:** Delete moves into a menu or the detail panel; a real pager.
- **Ads Campaigns:** an account switcher instead of repeating the KPIs and
  charts for every account.
- **Settings:** time selects for working hours; card widths aligned.
