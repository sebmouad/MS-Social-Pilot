# UI/UX audit — 2026-10-24

Status: **findings only, nothing implemented yet.** The owner chose to start
with batch 1 (quick fixes) and batch 2 (consistency). Each item below was
confirmed on a screenshot. Items marked "source checked" also have a confirmed
root cause in the code. Implementation, tests, a browser re-check and the
release are still to do.

## Evidence and method

- Local preview (synthetic seed data, dedicated database), dev server on
  `http://localhost:3300`. Database and seed setup are in [PREVIEW.md](PREVIEW.md).
  `scripts/dev/qa-db.sh` rebuilds the database.
- `scripts/dev/ui-audit.mjs` signs in as the preview QA user. For each route it
  takes a full-page screenshot and records DOM checks:
  - unnamed controls and unlabelled inputs;
  - horizontal overflow and elements past the right edge;
  - tap targets under 32px on phone;
  - raw `undefined`/`NaN` text and console errors.
- Coverage: 37 customer and Root routes at 1440px and 390px (75 captures).
- No page overflows horizontally at either width. The "unnamed switch" hits
  were false positives: `Switch` is labelled through `<label for>`.
- Preview-data artifact, not a bug: every composer channel shows "Reconnect",
  because the fake credential cannot be decrypted.

## Batch 1 — quick fixes (approved)

1. **Sidebar channel names clip without an ellipsis**, and the "All channels"
   highlight overruns its column. *Source checked:* `.ui-scope` and
   `.ui-scope__list` (`src/styles/shell.css` ~74–96) are grid items that keep
   `min-width: auto`. Fix with `grid-template-columns: minmax(0, 1fr)`, or
   `min-width: 0` on the rows.
2. **Approvals board overflows at 1440px.** *Source checked:* `.board` uses
   `repeat(4, minmax(280px, 1fr))`, and its breakpoints follow the viewport,
   not the content width (`publishing/ui/approvals/approvals.module.css`).
   Fix with auto-fit columns (`minmax(min(100%, 280px), 1fr)`) or a container
   query.
3. **Tasks and Agency show another area's tabs with nothing selected.**
   *Source checked:* `src/lib/hubs.ts` `hubPages` adds the Tasks and Agency
   pages only when the optional area is "used". The current page must always be
   present and selected.
4. **AI studio shows tabs that lead to "This is not available for your
   workspace right now."** *Source checked:* `AiStudioScreen.tsx` renders the
   `unavailable` empty state. Hide unusable tabs and redirect a stale `?tab=`.
5. **Plurals.** "1 failed posts" (Agency), "1 posts" (counts), "2 result(s)"
   (Ads Manage). Use one/other copy in EN and FR.
6. **Raw formats.** Ads Manage shows "EUR 92.4" while other Ads pages show
   "€92.40". ISO dates appear in Ads tables, in the range line, and on
   single-day chart axes. Use the shared locale formatters.

## Batch 2 — consistency (approved)

7. **One period control.** Insights pages use four different controls.
   Standardize on `DateRangePicker` with the `analytics.insightsPolicy`
   presets. Compare opens with empty dates; it should default to the policy
   period and the period before it.
8. **Filters.**
   - Live search instead of Search + Apply (Library, Ads Manage, Inbox).
   - Inbox stacks about 250px of filter UI, including an always-visible
     save-view form. Move it behind the Filter button and put saved views in a
     menu.
   - The default kind (Messages) should not count as an active filter.
9. **One primary button per view.** Settings › Channels shows two blue
   "Reconnect" buttons plus "Manage selections". Tasks shows "New task" twice.
10. **Shared components.** The native `<details>` triangle (Workspace "Link
    tagging (UTM)") and native sort selects (Insights Posts) should use the
    shared disclosure and `Select` styling.
11. **Disclaimers behind ⓘ.**
    - Ads Accounts export note.
    - Campaigns "levels overlap" and "Results differ by objective" notes,
      repeated for every ad account.
    - Settings › Channels footer.
12. **Agency KPI tiles.**
    - Seven tiles wrap and leave one orphan.
    - Labels wrap over four lines.
    - The icon chips are pink, off the brand-blue ladder.
    - The three Actions buttons wrap.

## Phone (390px) findings, not yet assigned to a batch

- **Tap targets under the 40px coarse-pointer goal:**
  - native checkboxes and radios at 13–16px (Queue, Team, Integrations scope
    matrix, Content feeds, Insights Compare);
  - inline text links at 16–20px ("Recipe planner", "Queue automation",
    "Choose ad accounts", "Open groups", "Review sent/failed posts",
    "Reset filters", Root sidebar links);
  - ⓘ buttons at 24px;
  - calendar Month/Week/List at 30px and event links at 28px.

  A shared coarse-pointer rule is the likely fix.
- **Ads › Analysis shows raw JSON.** The "Stored ratios, actions and
  additional fields" column renders a JSON `<pre>` to customers
  (`analytics/ad-objects/Screen.tsx` ~75). Use a labelled list instead.

## Batch 3 — redesigns (proposed, not approved)

- **Calendar:**
  - compact pills;
  - actions in a popover;
  - one brand "today" marker;
  - "+ New post" on hover or focus only;
  - one header row.
- **Approvals cards:**
  - notes only after "Request changes";
  - side-by-side actions;
  - author shown once;
  - meta line fixed.
- **Composer:**
  - Media right after Text;
  - a realistic neutral post preview built from module preview metadata;
  - visible reasons for blocked channels.
- **Inbox:** at most two badges per row, and a real pager.
- **Insights:**
  - empty and partial states on Overview;
  - Top posts duplicates;
  - a single day drawn as a bar;
  - human audience labels;
  - Posts metric columns with sortable headers.
- **Reports:** one onboarding empty state instead of five.
- **Library:** Delete moves into a menu or the detail panel; a real pager.
- **Ads Campaigns:** an account switcher instead of repeating the KPIs and
  charts for every account.
- **Settings:** time selects for working hours; card widths aligned.
