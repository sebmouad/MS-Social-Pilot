## New findings worth taking

1. **Inbox "Flux" (stream)** — one chronological stream mixing the selected
   accounts' published posts (with their comments) and incoming messages; type
   filter ("Tous les types"), "Show resolved" toggle, and the **same accounts
   column with 👁 show/hide** as Publishing (hidden accounts greyed). Second tab
   "Conversations" (threads). → confirms NEXT_PHASES Phase A (selection drives
   the inbox too) and adds: **resolved/unresolved state** on comments and
   messages, and a merged stream view. Note: their inbox is now open on the
   trial (it was locked on 10 Oct, `screens/33`).
2. **Email preferences = matrix per account × event**: content planner, new
   inbox message, analytics summary, queue is empty, draft updated, **publish
   failed**, **account disconnected** — each switchable per connected Page.
   → add to our Settings → Notifications (per member, per Page).
3. **Home dashboard**: a big "what do you want to do" prompt (AI), a
   **Publishing Queue** card (upcoming posts across all accounts, "Create post",
   "AI bulk planning", connected-accounts avatars + Add / Manage) and a
   **Publishing Operations** shortcut column (Analytics, Inbox with unread
   badge, Client review, Shared calendars). → our dashboard should lead with
   the upcoming queue for the current selection and shortcuts with counts.
4. **Riley scheduled tasks**: AI jobs on a schedule — "Weekly performance recap"
   (reads social analytics, writes a saved report + emails the highlights with a
   link, interprets rather than lists numbers), "Workflow review &
   recommendations", "Daily context refresh"; each has *Run now*, Active toggle,
   next run, run history. → idea for us: **AI weekly recap** attached to scheduled
   reports (needs the Anthropic key; plain-language "what changed and why" at the
   top of the emailed report).
5. **AI bulk posts** (`/social-media/ai-posts`): wizard — choose brand book →
   basic info → enhancements → personalise → advanced → generate; uses brand
   colours/fonts/voice. Ours: "Plan with AI" + Page voice; consider a stepper.
6. **AI Assistants & MCP**: setup page with per-assistant tabs (Claude, Codex,
   others), numbered steps, server URL to copy. Ours exists (API tokens + MCP);
   copy the **step-by-step per assistant** presentation.
7. **Usage page**: one credit balance for all AI features, "17 % used · 999 of
   6,000", reset date, 3-month history. → if AI costs matter, show AI usage per
   workspace the same way.
8. **Brand book**: company/customer/product context shared by AI and team.
   Ours: Page voice; could grow into a workspace brand book.
9. Workspace settings: name, id, time zone, **first day of week**, transfer
   ownership, danger zone. → we lack *first day of week* for the calendar.
10. Settings → Social accounts → **Planificateur** tab (queue slots) sits with
    the accounts; **Autorisations des comptes** (per-member access) — same as
    ours.
